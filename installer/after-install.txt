GitGrind is installed.

WHAT HAPPENS ON FIRST RUN
  A console window opens, prints a local address, and the app appears in its own
  window. The console is intentional: it is where the app reports its database
  location, question-bank health and any warnings. Closing it closes the app.

WHERE YOUR DATA LIVES
  Everything is in one file, next to the program:
      data\gitgrind.db
  Copy that file to back up your entire history. That is the whole backup
  procedure. Uninstalling does not delete it.

NOTHING ELSE IS REQUIRED
  No Python, no libraries, no account, no internet connection. The app never
  sends your data anywhere. The only network requests it can make are ones you
  explicitly start yourself.

THE QUESTION BANK
  This build ships with a curated set of questions. To load thousands more from
  your own GATE Overflow PDFs, see the "question bank" section of README.md.
  Questions whose answer is not printed in the source are kept and listed in the
  Bank tab, where one click searches for the answer and one keystroke saves it.

IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
  Open a terminal in the install folder and run:
      GitGrind.exe --check
  That prints the schema version, database size, bank health and topic coverage,
  which is enough to identify most problems.
